\begin{abstract}
We introduce a chroma-preserving low-light enhancement network that estimates a
smooth illumination field and conditions a gated fusion module on Retinex-derived
illumination priors. The method preserves chroma while lifting shadows at low
signal-to-noise ratios.
\end{abstract}
